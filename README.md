
# Tally — Bill Splitter

Tally is a simple web app to split bills fairly between friends.

**Try it now:**
👉 https://getsuzym.github.io/Tally/

## What you can do

- Add and remove participants
- Add dishes, assign who shared each dish
- Split the bill evenly or by dishes
- Apply tax and tip (percent or amount, before or after tax)
- Scan a receipt photo (English or Chinese) to auto-fill dish names, prices, and tax/tip (review before adding) — requires your own free Google Cloud Vision API key, pasted in once and saved only in your browser

No sign-up, no install — just open and use! Nothing is saved except your preferences and (if you use receipt scanning) your API key, kept locally in your browser. Scanning a receipt sends that photo to Google Cloud Vision for text recognition; everything else stays on your device.
